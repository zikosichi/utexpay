## Final prompt

Use case: product-mockup.
Asset type: a photorealistic EMPTY studio environment plate, to be composited underneath an existing HTML banking dashboard on a black landing page.

Input image 1 is a LIGHTING, MATERIAL and ATMOSPHERE REFERENCE ONLY. Its phone and every UI element are NOT wanted in the output.

Primary request: create the same rich, immersive warm backlighting, tactile charcoal floor and tasteful gold reflections seen around the reference phone, but render only the empty environment. We will place a large portrait glass dashboard into it afterward.

Composition: square image, high resolution. Camera at low product-photography height, looking very slightly down at a continuous dark studio floor. The floor begins softly at roughly 78 percent of the canvas height, without a sharp horizon line. The upper 75 percent is a deep black studio background. Broad, diffuse amber light behind the future object, extending vertically from the lower-left third toward the upper center. Another more restrained warm pool on the right. Light should remain visible at the LEFT AND RIGHT EDGES of the future object: reserve the central 60 percent width for a portrait dashboard that will be overlaid. Keep the light continuous and atmospheric, not shaped like an outline.

Lighting and materials: dramatic realistic studio product photography, luminous champagne highlights, gentle volumetric amber falloff, broad softbox reflections grazing finely textured satin-black stone. A warm gold pool runs across the floor near the lower fifth, with fine natural grain catching the light. The central foreground has a soft elongated reflected glow stretching toward the camera, with black between subtle reflections. Rich black tonal depth, sophisticated restrained contrast, tangible material detail, natural photographic falloff.

Integration: ALL FOUR OUTER EDGES must fall smoothly to nearly pure black; no bright rectangular image boundary. Floor detail concentrated near the center and lower center, fading into black along the bottom and outer corners. Main ambient light source feels strongest behind and to the upper-left of the future object. This is a realistic photograph of an empty lit studio, not a flat gradient or an abstract graphic.

STRICTLY NO OBJECTS: no phone, tablet, screen, dashboard, panel, card, pedestal, plinth, box, device silhouette, rim or frame. No text, letters, numbers, symbols, interface, logos, floating notifications or watermark. Do not render the product itself or its recognizable reflection. Only the empty lighting environment and its subtle ambient floor reflections.
